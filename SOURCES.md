## Identity and design

- https://scale-limited.bandcamp.com — Scale Limited profile: home to Greyscale & Redscale; founded in Lithuania in 2012 by producer and DJ grad_u and photographer Rima Prusakova; dub techno, ambient and deep electronic music; black-and-white analogue photography.
- The profile's public design data specifies white backgrounds, near-black text, a pale grey navigation bar, and black links. The official header pairs red Redscale typography with grey Greyscale typography. This prototype interprets those elements rather than reproducing the Bandcamp interface.

## Before public deployment

1. Confirm brand wording and permission to use the official artwork.
2. Recheck release status, dates, outgoing links and copy against the official catalogue.
3. Confirm final hosting and domain; add the correct canonical URL, absolute Open Graph URL/image, robots.txt and sitemap.xml.
4. Remove `noindex,nofollow` only when publication and indexing are approved.
5. Verify mobile/desktop rendering, accessibility and HTTP delivery on the production host.
6. Configure search engine discovery as appropriate; citations in AI search remain outside the site's control.
